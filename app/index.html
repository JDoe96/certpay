Wage determination rates

Enter each classification exactly as worded on the determination. Every worker is checked against these before you can file cleanly.
Required package = base rate + fringe. You may pay fringe in cash on the cheque or into a bona fide plan, but the two together must always meet the determination. The overtime premium is calculated on the base rate only — fringe is never multiplied. Paying 1.5× on the full package is the single most common overpayment; paying 1.5× on base but forgetting fringe entirely is the most common underpayment.

Crew

Pay & deductions

Gross comes from hours × rates. Enter the deductions actually withheld — net wages must tie to the cheque you issued.
Deductions beyond the standard ones — taxes, FICA, court-ordered support, authorised union dues — must be described on the certification. Anything that pushes a worker below the prevailing wage (tools, uniforms, transport, cash back) is a violation whether or not the worker agreed to it.

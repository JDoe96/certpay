The $500/month problem

Every contractor and sub on a federally funded job files a WH-347 every week, for every project. The DOL estimates it takes about 55 minutes per report by hand. The software that automates it starts at $300–$1,200 a month, and QuickBooks Payroll and Gusto don't produce the form at all. So a ten-person crew with one school-district job does it in a spreadsheet, on a Friday afternoon, badly.

!

It checks the maths, not just the format

Enter a worker at $30 base + $10 fringe against a $49.05 determination and CertPay tells you: short $9.05/hr on 40 hours — $362 owed. Most tools happily print the violation.

OT

Overtime the way Davis-Bacon actually works

The premium is 1.5× the base rate only. Fringe is paid on every hour and never multiplied. Getting this backwards is the classic back-wage finding, and CertPay gets it right.

CA

Real California support

Not a reskin. Daily overtime over 8 hours, double time over 12, the seventh consecutive day, SDI, cheque numbers and the full 13-column deduction grid the A-1-131 requires.

4

Catches full SSNs

Since 2009 the DOL requires only an identifying number — usually the last four SSN digits — never the full nine. If a complete SSN lands in that field, CertPay flags it as an error before you print.
